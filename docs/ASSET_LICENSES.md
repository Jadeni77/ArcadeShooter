# Asset source and license log

Record each third-party image, model, texture, sound, music track, font, animation, VFX asset, or package of creative assets **before** using it in a deliverable. Keep a copy or durable link to the license terms that applied when acquired. Paid assets and paid services require approval before introduction.

No third-party creative assets have been added for the game by the documentation task. The project contains Unity starter-template content; review its terms if any of that content is reused or redistributed outside the normal Unity project/build workflow. Self-made primitive placeholders do not need a third-party license entry, but identify them clearly in the project.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _Add one row per third-party asset or consistently licensed asset set_ | _e.g. audio, `Assets/Audio/...`_ | _Name_ | _URL, YYYY-MM-DD_ | _License and URL_ | _Check each right_ | _Exact required wording, or none_ | _Local license file or archived URL_ | _Approval/date if paid; restrictions_ |

Before importing, confirm the license covers the intended game platforms, distribution, modification, and any required credit. If the source or license cannot be verified, keep the asset out of the build until it can be resolved. Update a row when an asset is replaced, modified, or removed.
