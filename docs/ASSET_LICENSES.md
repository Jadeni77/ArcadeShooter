# Asset source and license log

Record each third-party image, model, texture, sound, music track, font, animation, VFX asset, or package of creative assets **before** using it in a deliverable. Keep a copy or durable link to the license terms that applied when acquired. Paid assets and paid services require approval before introduction.

The rifle, targets, effects, and three temporary sound clips added in M2 are self-made placeholders. The sound files were synthesized locally without third-party samples. The M3 melee enemy model, facing marker, and material are also made from Unity primitives and project-authored values; no third-party creative asset was added. M4–M6 likewise added only Unity primitives and project-authored materials, such as the remote player capsule and the mission sample and extraction markers; their world labels use the TextMesh Pro font listed below. M7 adds project-authored primitive rifle details and a third-person rifle. It imports no third-party art, animation, or sound. The optional model candidate in `STATUS.md` is research only and is not part of this deliverable. The project also contains Unity starter-template content; review its terms if any of that content is reused or redistributed outside the normal Unity project/build workflow.
