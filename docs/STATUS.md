- Review fixes on 2026-10-17. Each problem was first reproduced in a live two-player session, then retested:
  - A player standing on a wave spawn marker no longer stops the encounter and leaves the mission unable to finish. Waves fall back to open NavMesh ground near their markers, stay at least 2 m from every living player, and keep trying for up to 8 seconds. If a required zone still cannot continue, the mission ends in Failure with the reason shown, so the host can replay.
  - Scoring now reflects performance (see below). Previously every success scored 2,670 (A), or 2,800 (S) with the optional room-one kill.
  - Extraction labels inherited the flattened plate's scale and were sheared into unreadable streaks. Each marker is now an unscaled group holding a plate and an upright label.
  - The host rejected about one in five client shots fired at the rifle's full rate, because its fire-interval check allowed only 1 ms of network jitter. It now accepts a shot up to half an interval early while keeping the sustained 8 rounds per second.
  - A teammate who dies or disconnects inside the extraction area no longer restarts the countdown. Leaving the area still cancels it.
  - Replay and Return to Lobby no longer despawn avatars before the network load is accepted (NGO despawns them once the load starts), and a refused load is reported on the results screen.
  - Results number players in connection order (the host is Player 1, matching the spawn order), add a TEAM row (kills, shots, hits, accuracy, objectives), explain why the mission ended, and break down the team score.
  - A client the host removes now sees the host's reason and leaves the session. The host's own exit still reads "Host disconnected. Session ended."
  - The death message no longer says to leave to start a new match; a host who followed it would end the session for everyone.
- Scoring defaults: 100 per enemy kill, 10 per confirmed enemy hit, and 500 per shared objective counted once for the team. A successful extraction adds 4 points per second under a 5-minute par, up to 800 for team accuracy (enemy hits ÷ accepted shots), and up to 500 for the share of the starting squad that extracted. Failed runs earn no bonuses and always rank C. Successful runs rank B at 3,400, A at 3,900, and S at 4,300; lower scores rank C. See [MISSION_TESTING.md](MISSION_TESTING.md).

## Manual play test

Open `Assets/Scenes/MainMenu.unity` and press Play. `FirstPersonTest` is a networked scene with no player object of its own; its player and mission objects only spawn when a host starts it from the lobby, so pressing Play in it directly does not give a playable game.

For two players on one Mac, activate **Player 2** in **Window > Play Mode > Active Scenario**, or run two copies of a macOS build (see [MULTIPLAYER_TESTING.md](MULTIPLAYER_TESTING.md)). Press **Host Game**, enter the join code in the second player, press **Join Game**, then press **Start Game** on the host. Use WASD and mouse look to explore freely, Shift/Space to sprint and jump, the left mouse button to fire, R to reload, and E to collect the research sample. Clear Zone A in room two and Zone B in room three, collect the sample in room three, then gather every living player at the teal extraction marker in room one for ten seconds. Press Escape to release the cursor; click an empty part of the Game view to resume aiming. The full checklist is in [MISSION_TESTING.md](MISSION_TESTING.md).
