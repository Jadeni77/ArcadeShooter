## M4 danger zones and wave verification (historical local test)

- `DangerZone` uses a trigger collider and four states: Inactive, Warning, Active, and Cleared. Only a living player's `CharacterController` activates it. It activates once, continues when the player leaves, stops progression when that player dies, and resets with the scene. The warning and cleared messages have optional audio clips; none are assigned in the test scene. Scene gizmos show zone bounds.
- `EnemyWaveSpawner` owns serialized wave counts, delays, a melee enemy prefab, and spawn markers. It requires clear positions on the baked NavMesh before starting each wave, tracks only its spawned `EnemyHealth.Died` events, and advances only when its living set reaches zero. A failed configuration logs an error and stops the encounter. The scene has Zone A in room two (one wave of three) and Zone B in room three (waves of two and four, with a three-second interval). The separate encounter HUD shows warning, wave/total, remaining enemies, and a temporary cleared notice without covering health or ammo.

## M3 implementation and verification

- `MeleeEnemy` has Idle, Chase, Attack, and Dead states. A `NavMeshAgent` follows an acquired living player; a physics raycast prevents melee damage through walls. Detection radius, movement speed, attack range, damage, and cooldown are Inspector fields. `EnemyHealth` implements the existing `IDamageable` interface, and `EnemyFeedback` provides damage, attack, and death color cues. The model is made from Unity primitives.

Press R while dead, or use the component's **Restart Test Scene** context menu in Play mode, to reload the test scene. This is a local testing reset, not multiplayer respawn or revival.
